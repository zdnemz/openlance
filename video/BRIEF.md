## Intent

Concept #4, "The Contract, Typeset". An editorial promo for OpenLance, a
milestone-escrow freelance marketplace. A contract is set clause by clause in
Instrument Serif: each clause states one promise ("Clause 1. The money exists
before the work does."), and a real OpenLance app screen backs it as evidence.
No blockchain clichés: "enforced by a smart contract" appears only in the final
beat. Calm, assured voiceover, as if a narrator were reading a contract aloud.
It is a hybrid: motion typography plus captured app screens.

Draft clause spine (inferred, open to plan review):
1. Money locked before work starts (milestone escrow).
2. Released on proof of delivery.
3. Disagreements go to staked arbiters (SBT, ERC-5194).
4. No gas for your actions (sponsored meta-tx).
5. Reveal: enforced by a smart contract.
6. Logo + CTA: github.com/zdnemz/openlance
